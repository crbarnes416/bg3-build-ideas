BG3 Critical Hit Build

Creator: Mara Thornstep (fictional contributor)

Build Concept

This build is designed around generating as many attack rolls as possible during an encounter. Rather than relying on a single devastating strike, the strategy focuses on overwhelming enemies with repeated attacks and increasing the odds that one of those attacks becomes a critical hit.

Recommended Setup

  • Fighter 3 / Barbarian 9
  • Primary Ability: Dexterity
  • Combat Style: Aggressive melee combat
  • Role: Front-line critical hit specialist

Key Strategy

The goal is to land as many hits as possible in every encounter to maximize your chances of triggering critical hits. By staying in the middle of the action and continuously attacking, you create more opportunities for those high-damage moments that can quickly turn a battle in your favor.

Why We Like It

Landing critical hits is extremely satisfying, and this build is dedicated to making them happen as often as possible. With the right item setup, you can lower the dice roll required for a critical hit to as low as 14, dramatically increasing the frequency of those exciting damage spikes.
