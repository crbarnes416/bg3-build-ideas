BG3 Critical Hit Build

Creator: Mara Thornstep (fictional contributor)

Build Concept

This build focuses on landing as many critical hits as possible while maintaining steady combat performance. By combining high mobility with aggressive positioning, the character can consistently create opportunities for devastating attacks.

Recommended Setup

  • Class: Rogue 7 / Fighter 5
  • Primary Ability: Dexterity
  • Combat Style: Dual wielding finesse weapons
  • Role: Mobile damage dealer

Key Strategy

Open encounters from stealth whenever possible. Use mobility to isolate priority targets and focus burst damage on enemy spellcasters and ranged threats. Fighter levels provide additional attacks while rogue features reward careful positioning.

Why We Like It

The build remains effective throughout an entire playthrough and rewards players who enjoy tactical decision-making rather than standing still and exchanging blows.
