Lae'zel Build Guide

Author: Kiera Stoneblade (fictional contributor)

Build Theme

This guide turns Lae'zel into a heavily armored front-line warrior who combines the combat mastery of a Fighter with the divine power of a Paladin. The result is a durable companion capable of both absorbing punishment and dealing strong, consistent damage throughout the adventure.

Recommended Class Path

  • Fighter 9 / Paladin 3
  • Focus on Strength and Constitution
  • Use heavy armor and a powerful two-handed weapon
  • Role: Durable front-line damage dealer

Combat Approach

Lead the party into battle and hold the front line. Fighter levels provide the core combat abilities needed for repeated weapon attacks, while Paladin levels add valuable utility and additional conditions for when key targets need to be eliminated quickly.

Equipment Priorities

  • Prioritize heavy armor that improves survivability
  • Use the Silver Sword of the Astral Plane as the build's signature weapon
  • Hold on to items that grant exclusive bonuses to Githyanki characters and give them to Lae'zel whenever possible
  • Look for equipment that enhances mobility, defense, and weapon damage

Final Thoughts

With this build, Lae'zel gains superior defense through heavy armor while still delivering sufficient damage with her Silver Sword of the Astral Plane. Players who want a dependable front-line companion that remains effective from early encounters to the final battles will find this build especially rewarding.
